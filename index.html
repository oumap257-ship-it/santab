About Santa Barbara Resort

Your Ultimate Relaxation & Dining Haven in Webuye

Located conveniently in Webuye, Bungoma County, Santa Barbara Resort is renowned for its distinctive peach-and-terracotta architecture, expansive green lawns, and exceptional hospitality.

Whether you are traveling for business, stopping over along the highway, or looking for a peaceful weekend getaway with family and friends, our resort offers fully equipped B&B accommodation, a well-stocked bar, and mouthwatering local and continental cuisine.
